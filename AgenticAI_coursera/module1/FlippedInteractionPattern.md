# Flipped Interaction Pattern in Agentic AI

In **traditional AI interaction**, the user asks questions and the AI answers.

```text
User → Question → AI → Answer
```

In a **Flipped Interaction Pattern**, the roles are reversed:

```text
User → Goal
AI → Asks questions
User → Provides details
AI → Produces final solution
```

The AI takes the lead and gathers the information it needs before solving the problem. This makes the AI behave more like an **agent, consultant, interviewer, or assistant**, rather than a simple Q\&A system.

***

## Example 1: Travel Planning

### Traditional Interaction

```text
User: Plan a trip to Japan.
AI: Gives a generic itinerary.
```

### Flipped Interaction

```text
User: Help me plan a Japan trip.

AI: How many days are you traveling?
AI: What is your budget?
AI: Which cities do you want to visit?
AI: Do you prefer nature, shopping, or culture?
```

After collecting answers:

```text
AI: Here is your personalized 7-day Japan itinerary.
```

This produces a more tailored result because the AI first gathers context.

***

## Example 2: React Interview Preparation

Instead of:

```text
User: Give me React interview questions.
```

Use a flipped interaction:

```text
User: Help me prepare for a React interview.

AI: How many years of experience do you have?
AI: Is it frontend-only or full-stack?
AI: Is the interview focused on React, JavaScript, or system design?
AI: Are you targeting junior, mid-level, or senior roles?
```

Then the AI creates a customized preparation plan.

***

## Why It's Important in Agentic AI

Agentic systems don't always act immediately.

They:

1. Understand the goal.
2. Determine missing information.
3. Ask questions.
4. Gather context.
5. Plan actions.
6. Deliver the outcome.

This "asking before acting" behavior is a core characteristic of agent-like systems.

***

## Interview One-Liner

> **Flipped Interaction Pattern is an Agentic AI pattern where the AI takes control of the conversation by asking the user targeted questions to gather missing context before performing a task or generating a solution.**
